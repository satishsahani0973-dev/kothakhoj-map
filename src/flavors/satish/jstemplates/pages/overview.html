About

Hello! Welcome to KothaKhoj — a map to help students find rooms to rent without going house to house.

Looking for a room is hard. You walk street to street, ask around, and still miss places that are available. KothaKhoj puts it all on one map. See rooms for rent near your college, check the details, and contact the owner — all from your phone. Landlords and students can add a room to the map with its location, rent, and photos. Anyone searching can browse what's available, leave comments, ask questions, and share listings with friends.

Ready to find your room? Tap Get Started and explore the map.
