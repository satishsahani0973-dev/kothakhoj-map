Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This page explains what information KothaKhoj collects and how it is used. We keep it short and honest.

1. Information you give us

  • Account details — accounts for adding places (old students, colleges, and landlords) are created by KothaKhoj. Your username is shown publicly next to every place you add ("added by ..."). Choose a username you are happy to display.
  • Place details — the location, type, description, photos, availability date, and optional contact number you enter when adding a place. This information is shown publicly on the map by your own choice. Do not enter a phone number you are not willing to display publicly.
  • Comments — any comments and the name you choose to sign them with are public.

2. Information collected automatically

  • Session cookie — a small identifier that keeps you signed in to your account, and lets the site remember your submissions during your visit (for example, so you can delete a place you just added).
  • Browser storage — your "Continue browsing" choice on the welcome screen, and a list of the places you created (so they can be highlighted as yours), are stored in your own browser. They never leave your device.
  • Location — if you use the locate or directions feature, your device's location is used in your browser to center the map and calculate routes. Your live location is never stored on our servers.

3. Third-party services

The site uses Mapbox (map search and directions), Cloudflare (making the site fast and secure), and Google Analytics (counting visits so we know which pages help students most). When you use search or directions, coordinates are sent to Mapbox to calculate results, as is standard for any map service.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share your data with advertisers.
  • We do not send you marketing messages.
  • We do not track you across other websites.

5. Removing your information

You can delete a place you added using the delete button on it. If you have lost access to it, or want any information about you removed, email kothakhoj4@gmail.com and we will remove it.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
