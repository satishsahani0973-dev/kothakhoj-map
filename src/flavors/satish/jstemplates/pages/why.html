What is KothaKhoj for?

Some ways students and owners can use KothaKhoj:

  • find rooms and flats available for rent near your college
  • see the rent, location, and photos before you visit
  • compare different rooms on one map instead of walking house to house
  • contact the room owner directly
  • read comments and reviews from other students
  • owners can list an empty room and reach students fast
